# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately to:

`info@dreamweaversoffice.com`

Do not include customer data, credentials or destructive proof-of-concept activity in the initial report. Include the affected URL, a concise description, reproduction conditions and the potential impact.

The public machine-readable contact is available at `/.well-known/security.txt` after deployment.

## Scope note

This portfolio is a public marketing application. Contact delivery, analytics and any future CMS integration must be reviewed separately when their final providers are selected.
