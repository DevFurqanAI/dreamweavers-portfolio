# Client Content Required Before Public Launch

## Company approval

- Final company tagline and positioning statement
- Approved service summaries
- Confirmed head-office and studio details
- Approved social links
- Legal company name for structured data and privacy notice

## Case studies

For each project:

- Client name and approval to display it
- Project title and date
- Challenge
- Scope and services
- Technologies used
- Design and product screenshots
- Video or motion assets where available
- Verified outcomes and metrics
- Client testimonial and publication approval
- Live link and confidentiality restrictions

## Team

- Approved biographies
- LinkedIn URLs where desired
- Consistent high-resolution portraits
- Confirmation of spelling and title capitalization

## Legal and operations

- Final privacy policy
- Contact-form retention policy
- Form delivery provider
- Analytics and cookie decision
- Accessibility contact process
